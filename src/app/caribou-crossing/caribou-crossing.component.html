Caribou Crossing

Tap or click the left edge of the game to move the caribou left, the right edge to move right, and the middle to jump forward. Arrow keys work the same way.

A Frogger-inspired endless runner originally developed in 2014–2015 using libGDX and Java. The game shipped on Android and was available on the Google Play Store before being retired. This is a legacy portfolio piece — the HTML5 build runs here via GWT compilation of the original Java source. The leaderboard and achievement buttons connected to Google Play Games in the original release; those features are not active in this browser build.
